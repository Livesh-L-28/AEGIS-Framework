# Incidents Domain

Incidents represent detected reliability anomalies and lifecycle progression.

## Lifecycle States
- `OPEN`: Incident registered and awaiting investigation.
- `INVESTIGATING`: Evidence collection and causal reasoning underway.
- `MITIGATING`: Remediation actions proposed or in progress.
- `RESOLVED`: Incident mitigated and health criteria validated.
- `CLOSED`: Incident reviewed and archived.

## Severity Levels
- `LOW`
- `MEDIUM`
- `HIGH`
- `CRITICAL`
